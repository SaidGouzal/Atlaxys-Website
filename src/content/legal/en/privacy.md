## Who we are

This website is operated by **Atlaxys Consulting** ("Atlaxys", "we"), a company based in Morocco. For any question about this policy or your personal data, contact us at the email address shown on our [contact page](/en/contact/).

## What we collect

We only collect the personal data you choose to send us, and limited technical data needed to run the website:

- **Enquiry data** — when you use a contact or campaign form: your name, email address and, if you provide them, company, phone or WhatsApp number, country, project type, budget range, timeline and message.
- **Campaign attribution** — the page you arrived on and, if present in the link, campaign parameters (such as `utm_source`) and advertising click identifiers. These are stored in your browser for the current visit only and sent with your enquiry.
- **Messages sent by email or WhatsApp** — handled by those services under their own terms.
- **Analytics and advertising data** — only if you accept the corresponding cookies (see our [cookie policy](/en/legal/cookies/)).

## Why we use it

- To answer your enquiry and prepare a proposal (pre-contractual steps you requested).
- To measure the effectiveness of our website and campaigns — only with your consent.
- To protect the website against spam and abuse (legitimate interest).

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for automated decisions that affect you.

## Who receives it

Your enquiry is delivered to our team by email and/or through a workflow tool we configure for that purpose. Our service providers (hosting, email delivery, and — with your consent — analytics and advertising platforms) process data on our behalf. Some of them are located outside Morocco; where personal data is transferred abroad, we rely on the safeguards required by applicable law.

## How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not lead to a project are deleted or anonymised within 24 months. Data related to a client relationship is kept for as long as required by our contractual and legal obligations.

## Your rights

Under Moroccan Law 09-08 — and the GDPR, if you are in the European Union — you have the right to access, rectify and delete your personal data, and to object to its processing. You may also withdraw your consent to cookies at any time via "Cookie settings" in the footer. To exercise your rights, write to us via the contact page. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the CNDP (Commission Nationale de contrôle de la protection des Données à caractère Personnel) or your local data-protection authority.

## Security

We protect data with encrypted connections (HTTPS), restricted access, and providers that meet recognised security standards. No method of transmission is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures appropriate to the risk.

## Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top of the page shows the latest version.
