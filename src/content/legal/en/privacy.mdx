## Summary

- You can read this website without giving us any personal data. Like every website, our hosting provider processes technical connection data (such as your IP address) to deliver the pages.
- If you contact us through a form, by email, by phone or on WhatsApp, we use what you send only to answer you and, if you ask, to prepare a proposal.
- Analytics and advertising tools, when we use them, run **only after you accept them** in the cookie banner. Refusing is as easy as accepting, and you can change your mind at any time.
- We do not sell your personal data and we do not take automated decisions about you.

## Who is responsible for your data

The controller (in Moroccan law, the *responsable du traitement*) is **Atlaxys Consulting**, based in Morocco. Our full company details are on the [legal notice](/en/legal/legal-notice/) page. For any question about your data, write to the address shown in the "Contact and complaints" section below.

## Which laws apply

- **Morocco: Law 09-08** on the protection of individuals with regard to the processing of personal data, supervised by the **CNDP** (Commission Nationale de contrôle de la protection des Données à caractère Personnel). It applies to our processing because we are established in Morocco.
- **European Union / EEA (GDPR):** the GDPR can apply to a company outside the EU when it offers services to people in the EU or monitors their behaviour there (for example with analytics). Because our services and this website are also aimed at clients in Europe, we apply the GDPR's principles and rights to visitors and contacts located in the EU/EEA. Whether and how far it applies to a given processing is a legal question we keep under review.
- **United States:** US privacy laws vary by state and mostly apply only to businesses above revenue or volume thresholds. Based on our current size and activities, we do not believe we meet the thresholds of the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA) or similar state laws. We nonetheless honour the requests and signals described in "Your rights" below.

## What we do not do

- We do not sell personal data, rent it, or exchange it for anything of value.
- We do not send newsletters or marketing emails. If that changes, we will ask for your consent first.
- We do not take decisions about you based solely on automated processing.
- This website has no social-media plugins, embedded videos, maps or CAPTCHA services, and its fonts are hosted on our own server, so none of those providers receives data when you browse.
- What you type in the site search stays in your browser: the search runs on your device, and the text is not sent to us, to our hosting provider or to Google Analytics.

## Transfers outside Morocco and the EU

Some providers above are based in, or process data in, the United States or the European Union. Where personal data leaves Morocco we follow the transfer rules of Law 09-08 (transfers to countries recognised as providing adequate protection, or with the safeguards or authorisations the CNDP requires). ## Security

The website is served only over HTTPS, applies a strict Content Security Policy, loads no third-party code before you consent, and contains no user accounts or databases. Enquiries are accessible only to the people who handle them. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take measures appropriate to the risk and review them regularly.

## Your rights

**Everyone.** Whatever the law that applies to you, you can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal data, or to stop using it, by writing to us. You can withdraw consent to analytics or marketing at any time via "Cookie settings" at the bottom of every page. Withdrawing consent is as easy as giving it.

**Under Moroccan Law 09-08:** right to be informed, right of access, right of rectification (including deletion of inaccurate or unlawfully processed data), and right to object to processing on legitimate grounds, and to object to its use for prospecting without having to give reasons.

**If you are in the EU/EEA (GDPR):** right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability, objection (including to processing based on legitimate interests), and the right to withdraw consent without affecting earlier processing.

**United States residents:** although we believe we are currently below the thresholds of state privacy laws, we will answer requests to know, correct or delete personal data. When analytics or advertising tools are enabled, the advertising platforms receive identifiers and browsing events from this website, which some state laws treat as "sharing" or "targeted advertising". You can opt out at any time with "Cookie settings" or by turning on **Global Privacy Control** in your browser: we treat that signal as a refusal of marketing. We do not use sensitive personal information to infer characteristics about you.

**How to exercise your rights.** Write to us at the address below. We may ask for information to confirm your identity before acting on a request, and we answer without undue delay and within the time limits set by the applicable law (for the GDPR, one month, which can be extended in complex cases). Exercising your rights is free.
