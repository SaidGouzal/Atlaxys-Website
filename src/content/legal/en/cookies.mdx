## Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small files a website stores in your browser. "Similar technologies" include the browser's **local storage** and **session storage**, and scripts that send data about your visit to another company. This policy covers all of them; we call them "cookies" for short.

## Our approach

- **Nothing optional before you choose.** Analytics and advertising tools are not loaded, and set no cookies, until you accept their category. Continuing to browse, scrolling or closing the banner is never treated as consent.
- **Refusing is as easy as accepting.** The banner offers "Accept all", "Reject non-essential" and "Manage preferences", with the same visual weight for accepting and refusing.
- **Your choice is remembered for 180 days**, then you are asked again. It is also asked again if we add a new category of cookies.
- **Global Privacy Control.** If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we treat it as a refusal of marketing cookies.
- **Withdrawing is immediate.** When you switch a category off, we delete the cookies its tools set on this website and reload the page so their code stops running.

## Categories

**Strictly necessary:** needed to provide a function you asked for, such as remembering your cookie choice. These do not require consent and are never shared with third parties.

**Preferences:** remember a choice you made explicitly, such as the language you clicked in the language switcher or the light or dark theme.

**Analytics (consent):** measure how the website is used (pages viewed, traffic sources) so we can improve it.

**Marketing (consent):** attach the campaign that brought you here (utm parameters, ad click identifiers, referring site) to an enquiry you send, so we know which campaigns work. If we use advertising platforms such as Meta or LinkedIn, they are listed in the table above and in the consent settings; they measure the results of our ads and may link this information to an account you hold with them, under their own policies. When no advertising platform is listed, this category only stores the campaign information in your browser.

## Third-party cookies

Some advertising tools can also read or set cookies on their own domains (for example linkedin.com or facebook.com), especially if you are signed in to those services. We cannot read or delete those cookies; you can manage them in your browser or in your privacy settings on those platforms.

## What we do not use

No social-media "like" or "share" plugins, no embedded videos or maps, no CAPTCHA, no third-party fonts (our fonts are served from our own website) and no device fingerprinting.

## Changing your choice

- Use **"Cookie settings"** at the bottom of any page to accept, refuse or withdraw each category at any time. (This link appears only when the website uses optional cookies.)
- You can also delete cookies and site data, or block them, in your browser settings. Blocking strictly necessary storage only means we cannot remember your choice.

Refusing optional cookies does not affect your ability to use the website or to contact us.
