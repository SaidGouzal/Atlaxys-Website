## Our approach

This website works without any optional cookies. Analytics and advertising tools are **only loaded after you accept them**, and you can change your choice at any time from "Cookie settings" in the footer.

## Strictly necessary

These are stored in your browser's local storage, not shared with third parties, and do not require consent:

| Name | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| `atlaxys-consent` | Remembers your cookie choice | Until you change it |
| `atlaxys-lang` | Remembers a language you picked explicitly | Until cleared |
| `atlaxys-attribution` | Keeps campaign information for the current visit, sent only with an enquiry | Current browser session |

## Analytics (optional)

If enabled and accepted, we use **Google Analytics** and/or **Google Tag Manager** to understand how visitors use the site — which pages are read and where visitors come from — in aggregated form.

## Marketing (optional)

If enabled and accepted, we use the **Meta Pixel** and the **LinkedIn Insight Tag** to measure the results of our advertising campaigns on those platforms.

## Managing your choice

- Use "Cookie settings" in the footer to accept or refuse each category.
- You can also delete cookies and site data from your browser settings.

Refusing optional cookies does not affect your ability to use the website or to contact us.
