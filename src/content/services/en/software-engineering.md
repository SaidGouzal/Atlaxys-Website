## When this is the right service

You have a product to launch, a process that has outgrown spreadsheets, or a system that customers or staff depend on every day. You need more than a website and less than a permanent in-house department — or you have a team and need senior engineers to carry a critical piece.

## How a build runs

Every engagement follows the same backbone: **discover, architect, build, ship, operate**. What changes is the size of each step.

1. **Discovery** — workshops with the people who will use the system, a review of whatever exists today, and a first scope with risks named.
2. **Architecture** — a short, written design: data model, integrations, hosting, security model and the trade-offs we chose. You approve it before code is written.
3. **Build** — one- to two-week iterations. Every iteration ends with working software on a staging environment you can click through.
4. **Ship** — automated tests and deployment pipeline, monitoring and alerting, a launch checklist and a rollback plan.
5. **Operate** — support, maintenance and the next roadmap, or a documented handover to your team.

## What "production-ready" means to us

- **Observable**: structured logs, error tracking and uptime checks from the first release.
- **Secure by default**: least-privilege access, secrets outside the codebase, dependency updates on a schedule.
- **Fast**: performance budgets for pages and API endpoints, measured rather than assumed.
- **Accessible**: interfaces that work with a keyboard and a screen reader.
- **Documented**: a README that lets a new engineer run the project in under an hour.

## Working with clients in Morocco and abroad

We build for Moroccan businesses — including interfaces in French and Arabic with proper right-to-left support — and for international companies that want an engineering partner in a nearby time zone. Contracts, documentation and daily communication happen in English, French or Arabic.
