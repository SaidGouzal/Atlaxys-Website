## When to bring us in

- **Before a big build** — to validate architecture, scope and estimates, and avoid expensive wrong turns.
- **When something is slowing down** — releases, performance, costs, or the team's ability to change the code.
- **Before an investment or acquisition** — technical due diligence that investors and buyers can rely on.
- **When choosing a partner** — to write the brief and assess proposals from development agencies, including us.

## How an engagement works

1. **Context** — interviews with leadership and the engineering team, access to code, infrastructure and metrics.
2. **Assessment** — hands-on review of the system and the way it is built and run.
3. **Recommendations** — a written report with findings ranked by risk and effort, a target architecture where relevant, and a practical roadmap.
4. **Follow-through** — optional support while your team implements, or implementation by our engineers.

## Independent by default

When we advise on vendor selection or review another team's work, we say so if we have a potential conflict of interest, and we evaluate against written criteria agreed with you in advance.
