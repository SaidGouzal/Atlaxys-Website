## When to bring us in

- **Before a large project**, to check the architecture, the scope and the estimates, and avoid expensive wrong turns.
- **When things are slowing down**: releases, performance, costs, or your team's ability to change the code.
- **Before an investment or an acquisition**, to give investors or buyers a clear technical picture of the system (technical due diligence).
- **When choosing a provider**, to write the brief and assess proposals from development agencies, including ours.

## How an engagement works

1. **Context.** Interviews with management and the development team, and access to the code, hosting and figures.
2. **Assessment.** A hands-on review of the system and of how it is built and run.
3. **Recommendations.** A written report with findings ranked by risk and effort, a target architecture where relevant, and a practical roadmap.
4. **Follow-through.** Optional support while your team implements the plan, or implementation by our team.

## Independent by default

When we help you choose a provider or review another team's work, we tell you upfront about any potential conflict of interest, and we assess against written criteria agreed with you in advance.
